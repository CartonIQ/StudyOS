## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Planner** — Organize your study schedule and tasks.
- 🔄 **Revision** — Spaced repetition system to remember everything.
- ⏱️ **Focus Mode** — Pomodoro-style timer to eliminate distractions.
- 📝 **Notes** — Keep your study materials organized by subject.
- 🎓 **Exams & Quizzes** — Test your knowledge offline.
- 🔒 **100% Offline** — Zero servers. Zero tracking. Your data never leaves your phone.
